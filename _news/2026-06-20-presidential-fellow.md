Prof. Sriraam Natarajan has been appointed as the Presidential Distinguished Fellow for Artificial Intelligence at The University of Texas at Dallas.

This prestigious appointment recognizes Prof. Natarajan's leadership and contributions to the field of artificial intelligence, as well as his commitment to advancing impactful research and fostering global collaboration. Through these collaborations with researchers, institutions, and organizations across the globe, Prof. Natarajan is helping build meaningful connections that advance AI research, promote the development of AI systems, and create valuable opportunities for the next generation of scientists and innovators.

We congratulate Prof. Natarajan on this well-deserved recognition and look forward to the continued impact of his leadership in shaping the future of artificial intelligence.
