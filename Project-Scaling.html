Scaling

Mayukh Das, Devendra Singh Dhami

A major bottleneck to scalable Statistical Relational AI is counting — computing the number of satisfied groundings of a first-order logic formula, which is #P-complete and grows increasingly costly as databases scale. Yet exact counts rarely matter at that scale: a professor's popularity barely changes whether their papers have 400 or 419 citations.

We develop performance-preserving approximation strategies for these counts, using graph-database techniques to make structure search, likelihood estimation, and lifted inference tractable at scale, without sacrificing the accuracy that matters.
