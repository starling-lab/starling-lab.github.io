Dataset

Drug-Drug Interactions

Five heterogeneous similarity measures used to predict drug-drug interactions.

Scale

196 drugs · 19,110 pairs

Author

Devendra Dhami

Overview

The data consists of five similarity matrices used for drug-drug interaction prediction: reachability (paths between drugs in a knowledge graph of chemical reactions), molecular feature similarity, SMILES string similarity, molecular fingerprint similarity, and MACCS fingerprint similarity.

All matrices are symmetric across 196 drugs, corresponding to 19,110 possible interactions.
