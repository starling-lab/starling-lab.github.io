QuaKE: Extracting Qualitative Knowledge for Early Prediction of Gestational Diabetes

Athresh Karanam, Alexander L. Hayes, Harsha Kokel, David M. Haas, Sriraam Natarajan · AIME 2021

Statements like "risk of GDM increases with BMI" describe a monotonic influence between variables — concise, general knowledge that's hard to express as conjunctive decision rules. Rather than asking experts to supply these statements up front, we ask the reverse question: given a learned causal or probabilistic model, can we reverse-engineer this kind of qualitative knowledge automatically?

QuaKE defines metrics for the degree of monotonic and synergistic influence between variables, then extracts qualitative rules from a learned model. Applied to predicting gestational diabetes from clinical observations, the extracted rules matched a clinical expert's own judgment with 0.923 precision, with the mismatches pointing to promising directions for future study.
