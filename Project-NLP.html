Natural Language Processing

Alexander L. Hayes, Kaushik Roy

Traditional machine learning models struggle with natural language because they tend to ignore relationships between sentences, words, and text within a document — forcing careful, brittle feature construction and unrealistic conditional-independence assumptions that don't scale to large text corpora.

Methods that extract relations from text and exploit them directly during learning and inference sidestep both problems, and produce robust, interpretable predictions. We've applied BoostSRL to extracting relations from clinical notes, adverse drug event mentions, and financial documents.
