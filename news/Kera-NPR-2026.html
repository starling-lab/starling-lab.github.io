KERA/NPR Interview — Prof. Sriraam Natarajan

Prof. Sriraam Natarajan discusses current concerns around AI safety, arguing that many recent failures reflect insufficient oversight, poorly specified tasks, and weak safeguards rather than autonomous intent on the part of AI systems.

Listen

Overview

Dr. Natarajan discusses concerns around AI safety, emphasizing that many current risks stem from insufficient oversight, weak safeguards, and poorly specified tasks rather than AI systems acting with independent intent. He also highlights the importance of responsible AI education, human supervision, and independent testing and regulation to ensure that increasingly capable systems are deployed safely.
