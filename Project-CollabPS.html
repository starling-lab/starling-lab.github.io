Human-guided Collaborative Problem Solving

Harsha Kokel, Mayukh Das, Sriraam Natarajan, and collaborators · ICAPS 2021 / ACS 2022

Lara is our integrated system of learning, reasoning, and communication. We frame human-machine collaborative problem solving as a planning task coupled with natural-language communication: two players, an architect (human) and a builder (machine), collaborate to build target structures in a Minecraft environment via chat.

The architect sees the target structure and must describe it to the builder, who cannot see it directly. Success requires bi-directional communication, the builder's ability to seek clarification, and a shared vocabulary that both players expand through experience. Lara combines a natural-language engine, a concept learner that induces generalized concepts from limited interaction, and a planner that acts on human instruction.
